Questions

Good to know.

Do you keep my voice recordings?

Your voice is turned into text and then discarded.

Only the text of that day's list is kept, and recordings are capped at two minutes.

Does it connect to my calendar?

Not yet, and on purpose. Tell it about fixed things in the same sentence, like "meeting at 2", and the day is planned around them.

What if it guesses a duration wrong?

Every estimate is marked until you set it. Tap it, change the time, and only the unfinished part of the day is re-planned.

What happens to things I don't get to?

Move them to tomorrow, a weekday or a date you pick. They show up as carried over when you start that day.

Which phones does it work on?

Any modern phone browser. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, and today's plan is available offline.
